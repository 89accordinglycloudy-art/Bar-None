House Rules

  1. First rule of BAR NONE: you do not talk about BAR NONE. (But we really, really want you to talk about BAR NONE.)
  2. Race to 8 bars. Every turn you draw a sound card and an action card.
  3. Play the sound and the shared beat bends for everybody — or bin it. Playing it draws you an extra card.
  4. One prompt card and one dirty trick per turn. Write your bar off the prompt before the timer dies — and when it dies, whatever you've typed is what you wrote. Locked is locked.
  5. Sabotage lands. A shield blocks one hit. An empty bar still counts — it just counts as a freeze, and the room remembers.
  6. First writer to 8 bars triggers the last round. Then the room votes on the verses. Talk is cheap. Bars decide.
