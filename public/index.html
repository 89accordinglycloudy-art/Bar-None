House Rules

  1. First rule of BAR NONE: the clock decides. Run out of time and the game moves without you — whatever you typed is what you wrote.
  2. Race to 8 bars. Every turn you draw a sound card and an action card.
  3. Play the sound and the shared beat bends for everybody — or bin it. Playing it draws you an extra card.
  4. One prompt card and one dirty trick per turn. Write your bar off the prompt before the timer dies. Locked is locked.
  5. Sabotage lands. A shield blocks one hit. An empty bar still counts — it just counts as a freeze, and the room remembers.
  6. First fighter to 8 bars triggers the last round. Then the room votes on the verses. Talk is cheap. Bars decide.
